01 / A little about me

Good engineering begins with curiosity and ends with purpose.

I am a fourth-year civil engineering student at the University of British Columbia Okanagan, with a particular interest in structural engineering, construction, and project management.

Throughout my undergraduate degree, I have sought opportunities beyond the classroom to develop my technical abilities, challenge myself, and grow as a leader.

I am especially interested in how projects evolve from an initial idea into something tangible, and how collaboration between people makes that transformation possible.

As I approach graduation, I hope to contribute to meaningful infrastructure projects while continuing to develop as an engineering professional.

03 / Beyond Engineering

Life beyond engineering.

There is more to me than engineering. I believe the experiences we have outside our profession shape the perspectives, creativity, and character we bring into it.

Challenging myself.

I enjoy pushing myself beyond my comfort zone. Training for my first triathlon has taught me to appreciate the small steps, embrace challenges, and enjoy the process of becoming better at something new.

Staying curious.

Curiosity has been a part of who I am for as long as I can remember. From exploring the wilderness to painting and photography, I love discovering new places, perspectives, and ways of expressing creativity.

Bringing people together.

Whether it's hosting dinners, organizing events, or spending time with family and friends, I find joy in bringing people together. I value meaningful connections and creating spaces where everyone feels a sense of belonging.
